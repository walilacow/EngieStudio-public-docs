喵喵擠痘痘 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

一句話說明：喵喵擠痘痘不收集、不上傳、不分享你的任何個人資料。遊玩紀錄、成就與設定都只存在你自己的裝置裡。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有伺服器，不連網，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容。

2. 資料存在哪裡

你的遊玩進度（遇過哪些臉、擠了幾顆）、成就、統計、玩到一半的那張臉與設定（音效、震動、血腥程度等），都只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

如果你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，這些資料可能會包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中，由 Apple 依照你的設定處理。

3. 我的臉

「做一張像我的臉」會開啟系統的選照片視窗，讓你挑一張照片；這個視窗由 iOS 提供，App 不需要相簿權限，也看不到你沒選的照片。

你選的照片只在這台裝置上分析，取出平均膚色與髮色後就丟棄，不會儲存照片、不會上傳。App 只記住這兩個顏色，用來產生一張卡通臉；你可以在設定裡隨時刪除。

4. App 會要求的權限

本 App 不使用相機、麥克風、定位或通知。

只有你在「最爽一爆」回放的分享面板按「儲存影片」時，iOS 才會詢問「加入照片」權限，把那段回放影片新增到你的相簿；App 不會讀取相簿裡的其他照片。不允許也可以照常遊玩。觸覺回饋（震動）由系統直接提供，不涉及任何資料。

5. 購買

本 App 為一次付費買斷，透過 Apple 的 App Store 完成；付款與帳號資料由 Apple 處理，我們收不到你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple 帳號資訊。App 內沒有廣告，也沒有額外購買項目。

6. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

7. 政策更新

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

Meow Pimple Pop Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Meow Pimple Pop does not collect, upload or share any of your personal data. Your progress, achievements and settings stay on your device.

1. We do not collect data

The app has no accounts and no servers, never connects to the internet, and does not use any advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We (EngieStudio) cannot see anything you do in the app.

2. Where your data lives

Your play progress (which faces you have met, how many blemishes you have popped), achievements, statistics, the face you left unfinished and your settings (sound, haptics, gore level and so on) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in Apple's device backup and handled by Apple according to your settings.

3. My Face

"Make a Face Like Mine" opens the system photo picker so you can choose one photo. The picker is provided by iOS, so the app needs no photo library permission and cannot see any photo you do not choose.

The photo you choose is analyzed only on this device. The app takes its average skin and hair colors and then discards the photo; it never saves or uploads it. Only those two colors are kept to build a cartoon face, and you can delete it in Settings at any time.

4. Permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or notifications.

Only when you tap "Save Video" in the share sheet of the Best Pop replay does iOS ask for permission to add photos, so the replay video can be added to your photo library. The app never reads any other photos in your library. You can keep playing if you decline. Haptic feedback is provided by the system and involves no data.

5. Purchases

The app is a one-time paid purchase through Apple's App Store. Payment and account details are handled by Apple; we never receive your name, payment method or Apple Account information. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the app's release notes.
