怎麼玩

基本玩法每一關是一張可愛的臉，上面藏著幾顆粉刺和痘痘。放大找到它們：白頭點一下就破，黑頭、膿皰和囊腫要按住畫小圈揉到爆開。全部清完就換下一張，沒有時限、不會失敗。
痘痘好小，找不到？這是故意的。雙指捏合或雙擊可以放大臉；畫面上方的「剩 N 顆」告訴你還有幾顆；10 秒沒擠到，剩下的會淡淡閃一下（可在設定關掉）。
揉了沒反應？膿皰和囊腫要按在痘痘上原地畫小圈，不要畫到痘痘外面；囊腫要連續揉、中途不要放手。第一次玩的教學可以在「設定 → 再看一次教學」重看。
不想看到血？「設定 → 血腥程度」選「溫和」，囊腫只會流膿不流血。第一次開始遊戲時也會先問你。
沒有聲音或震動？到「設定」確認音效已開、震動強度不是「關」。震動要裝置支援觸覺回饋（iPhone）。

遊戲內容

會重複嗎？臉庫有 12,000 張不同的臉：人臉之外還有貓、狗、兔子、熊、豬、倉鼠六種動物臉，100 張稀有的妖怪臉，和幾張藏起來的彩蛋臉。全部遇過一輪才會重新洗牌；同一張臉再遇到時，難度和痘痘位置會不一樣。
痘王是什麼？每清完 10 張臉，下一張會出現一顆超大的痘王，要揉很久才會破；放開手不會重來。
連鎖爆、黑頭貼？靠得很近的膿皰和囊腫，爆開一顆可能把旁邊的一起帶爆。困難和地獄難度的人臉有時會在鼻子貼一片黑頭貼，按住往上撕，太快會斷。
我的臉「設定 → 做一張像我的臉」可以選一張照片，App 在這台裝置上取出膚色和髮色，做一張像你的卡通臉，之後每 20 張出現一次。照片不會被儲存或上傳；可以在設定裡重做或刪除。
最爽一爆、瘋狂 30 秒、膿量博物館清完一張臉後，摘要上的播放鍵可以看最大一顆爆開的慢動作回放，也能分享或存成影片。首頁的「瘋狂 30 秒」是限時擠痘模式（可在設定隱藏）。「成就與統計」裡的膿量博物館會把你擠出的膿量裝進瓶子收藏。
資料會不會不見？進度、成就、統計都存在這台裝置上；換裝置不會自動同步。刪除 App 會一併刪除。詳見隱私權政策。

How to play

The basicsEach round is a cute face with a few blemishes hidden on it. Zoom in to find them: tap a whitehead to pop it, and press and rub small circles on blackheads, pustules and cysts until they burst. Clear them all to move to the next face. No timer, no failing.
The blemishes are tiny, I can't find them.That's on purpose. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in; the counter at the top shows how many are left; after 10 seconds without a pop, the remaining ones flash faintly (you can turn this off in Settings).
Rubbing does nothing.Pustules and cysts need small circles right on the blemish, not around it. Cysts need continuous rubbing without lifting your finger. You can replay the tutorial from Settings → Replay Tutorial.
I'd rather not see blood.Settings → Gore level → Gentle: cysts release pus only. The app also asks you the first time you play.
No sound or haptics?Check Settings: Sound on, and Haptic Strength not set to Off. Haptics need a device with haptic feedback (iPhone).

What's inside

Does it repeat?There are 12,000 different faces: people, six kinds of animals (cat, dog, rabbit, bear, pig and hamster), 100 rare monster faces and a few hidden Easter-egg faces. They reshuffle only after you have met them all, and the same face gets a different difficulty and layout the next time.
What is the Pimple King?After every 10 cleared faces, the next one has a giant Pimple King that takes a long rub to pop. Letting go won't reset it.
Chains and blackhead strips?Pustules and cysts that sit close together can pop each other in a chain. On Hard and Nightmare, a human face sometimes has a blackhead strip on the nose: hold it and peel upward, but not too fast or it tears.
My FaceSettings → Make a Face Like Mine lets you pick a photo. The app reads the skin and hair colors on this device and builds a cartoon face that looks like you, which shows up every 20 faces. The photo is never saved or uploaded, and you can redo or delete the face in Settings.
Best Pop, Crazy 30s and the Pus MuseumAfter you clear a face, the play button on the summary shows a slow-motion replay of the biggest pop, which you can share or save as a video. Crazy 30s on the home screen is a timed popping mode (you can hide it in Settings). The Pus Museum in Achievements & Stats collects the pus you have popped in jars.
Will I lose my data?Progress, achievements and statistics live on this device only and do not sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes them. See the privacy policy.
