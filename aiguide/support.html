怎麼讀

從哪一章開始？第一次打開時會問你三個簡單問題，幫你找到適合的起點；也可以略過，從第一章開始。所有章節都能自由閱讀，不會鎖住。想重新找起點，到「設定 → 重新做定位」。
字太小、太擠？「設定 → 閱讀設定」可以調字級、主題（淺色／深色／跟隨系統）和行距。App 也支援 iPhone／iPad 系統的「更大字體」。
不想自己看，想用聽的？閱讀畫面上方的朗讀按鈕會把這一章唸給你聽，鎖定螢幕也能繼續。語音和速度在「設定 → 朗讀」調整。開啟 VoiceOver 時不會顯示朗讀按鈕，請直接用 VoiceOver 閱讀。
看到不懂的詞？點文章裡標示的術語，會跳出白話解釋；全部術語也可以在「工具 → 術語表」查。
動畫看了會暈？「設定 → 互動動畫改為靜態」會把互動動畫換成一步一步的靜態圖。

測驗與等級

章末小測驗每章最後有幾題小測驗，答錯的題目會收進「錯題回顧」，可以再練習。測驗只是幫你確認有沒有讀懂，不影響能不能往下讀。
升級測驗與徽章每個等級都有升級測驗，通過就能取得那一級的徽章，從「麻瓜」一路到「專家」。沒通過可以隨時重考。

工具

提示詞範本書裡用到的提示詞範本都整理在「工具 → 提示詞範本」，按「複製」就能貼到你常用的 AI App 裡使用。
書籤與搜尋閱讀時可以加書籤，之後在「工具 → 書籤」找回來；「工具 → 搜尋」可以搜全書內容。

資料與網路

需要網路嗎？不需要。全書內容、動畫、測驗和朗讀都在手機裡，沒有網路也能讀。App 不會連到任何 AI 服務；要實際試用 AI，請照書中說明使用你自己的 AI App。
資料會不會不見？進度、測驗紀錄、書籤都存在這台裝置上；換裝置不會自動同步。刪除 App 會一併刪除。想從頭開始，到「設定 → 重設閱讀進度」。詳見隱私權政策。
中英文切換App 會跟隨系統語言；也可以在「設定 → 內容語言」單獨切換書的語言。

iPhone、iPad（iOS／iPadOS 17 以上）。

Questions or suggestions? Email walilacow.hu@gmail.com with the app version (Settings → About), your device model and OS version, and a screenshot if possible.

Reading

Where should I start?The first time you open the app, three simple questions help you find a good starting point. You can also skip them and start from Chapter 1. Every chapter is open; nothing is locked. To find a new starting point, go to Settings → Retake the starting-point questions.
Text too small or crowded?Settings → Reading settings lets you change text size, theme (light, dark or follow system) and line spacing. The app also supports the larger text sizes in iPhone and iPad settings.
Rather listen than read?The read-aloud button at the top of the reader reads the chapter to you, even with the screen locked. Change the voice and speed in Settings → Read aloud. The button is hidden while VoiceOver is on; use VoiceOver to read instead.
Found a word you don't know?Tap a highlighted term for a plain-language explanation, or browse them all in Tools → Glossary.
Animations make you dizzy?Settings → Show animations as still steps replaces the interactive animations with step-by-step still images.

Quizzes and levels

Chapter quizzesEach chapter ends with a short quiz. Questions you miss go into Review mistakes so you can practice them again. Quizzes only help you check your understanding; they never stop you from reading on.
Level quizzes and badgesEach level has a level quiz. Pass it to earn that level's badge, all the way from Newcomer to Expert. You can retake it any time.

Tools

Prompt templatesThe prompt templates from the book are collected in Tools → Prompt templates. Tap Copy and paste them into the AI app you use.
Bookmarks and searchAdd bookmarks while reading and find them again in Tools → Bookmarks. Tools → Search looks through the whole book.

Data and internet

Do I need the internet?No. The whole book, animations, quizzes and read aloud are on your device and work offline. The app never connects to any AI service; to try AI for real, follow the book and use your own AI app.
Will I lose my data?Progress, quiz records and bookmarks live on this device only and do not sync between devices. Deleting the app deletes them. To start over, use Settings → Reset reading progress. See the privacy policy.
Switching languagesThe app follows your system language, and you can change the book's language separately in Settings → Content language.

Supported devices

iPhone and iPad (iOS/iPadOS 17 or later).
