AI 入門指南 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

一句話說明：AI 入門指南不收集、不上傳、不分享你的任何個人資料。閱讀進度、測驗紀錄、書籤與設定都只存在你自己的裝置裡。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有伺服器，閱讀時不需要連網，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容。

書裡會教你怎麼使用各種 AI 服務，但 App 本身不會連到任何 AI 服務，也不會把你輸入或閱讀的內容送出去。

2. 資料存在哪裡

你的閱讀進度、章末測驗與升級測驗紀錄、錯題、徽章、書籤、首次使用時的定位結果與閱讀設定（字級、主題、行距、內容語言、朗讀語音與速度等），都只存在你的裝置上。刪除 App 就會一併刪除；也可以在「設定 → 重設閱讀進度」清除進度、測驗紀錄、錯題與徽章，並選擇要不要一起刪除書籤。

如果你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，這些資料可能會包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中，由 Apple 依照你的設定處理。

3. 朗讀功能

朗讀使用 iOS 內建的語音，在裝置上把文字唸出來，不會把文字送到網路上。為了讓你鎖定螢幕或切到其他 App 時還能繼續聽，App 會使用背景播放音訊。

4. App 會要求的權限

本 App 不使用相機、麥克風、相簿、定位、聯絡人或通知，也不會要求這些權限。

你按下範本的「複製」時，文字會放進系統剪貼簿，方便你貼到其他 App；App 不會讀取剪貼簿裡的其他內容。

5. 外部連結

書中部分章節附有參考網站的連結，「關於」頁也有隱私權政策、使用說明與支援的連結；點按後會用瀏覽器開啟該網站，之後就由該網站依它自己的隱私權政策處理。問題回報會開啟你的郵件 App。這些操作只在你主動點按時發生，App 本身不會傳送任何資料。

6. 價格

本 App 免費，沒有廣告、內購或訂閱。

7. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

8. 政策更新

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

AI Starter Guide Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

In short: AI Starter Guide does not collect, upload or share any of your personal data. Your reading progress, quiz records, bookmarks and settings stay on your device.

1. We do not collect data

The app has no accounts and no servers, does not need the internet for reading, and does not use any advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We (EngieStudio) cannot see anything you do in the app.

The book teaches you how to use various AI services, but the app itself never connects to any AI service and never sends what you read or type anywhere.

2. Where your data lives

Your reading progress, chapter and level quiz records, missed questions, badges, bookmarks, first-run placement result and reading settings (text size, theme, line spacing, content language, read-aloud voice and speed and so on) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also use Settings → Reset reading progress to clear progress, quiz records, mistakes and badges, and choose whether to delete bookmarks too.

If you use iCloud or computer backups, this data may be included in Apple's device backup and handled by Apple according to your settings.

3. Read aloud

Read aloud uses the voices built into iOS and speaks the text on your device; the text is never sent over the internet. The app uses background audio so you can keep listening with the screen locked or while using another app.

4. Permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications, and never asks for these permissions.

When you tap Copy on a template, the text is placed on the system clipboard so you can paste it into another app. The app never reads anything else on your clipboard.

5. External links

Some chapters include links to reference websites, and the About page links to this privacy policy and Help & support. Tapping a link opens that website in your browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies. Report a problem opens your mail app. These happen only when you tap them; the app itself never sends any data.

6. Price

The app is free, with no ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the app's release notes.
