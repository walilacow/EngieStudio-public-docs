喵喵即時口譯 使用說明與支援

有問題或建議，請寄信到 walilacow.hu@gmail.com，並附上手機型號與 iOS 版本，我們會盡快回覆。

怎麼使用

開啟 App，選好要聽的語言與要翻成的語言，按「開始」並允許麥克風與語音辨識。把手機放在聲音來源旁邊（例如電腦開會的喇叭旁），字幕會即時出現在畫面上，下方是翻譯。顏色較淡的字還在修正中，變深就代表已經確定。

常見問題

可以直接收電腦或視訊軟體的聲音嗎？
不行。iOS 不允許 App 直接取得其他 App 的聲音，本 App 只能透過麥克風收音。如果你用同一支手機開視訊會議，視訊軟體可能佔用麥克風，建議用另一台裝置收音。

第一次使用要等很久，或顯示需要下載語言？
第一次使用某個語言時，iOS 需要下載該語言的資料，請在有網路的環境下完成下載。下載完成後可以離線使用。日文與韓文辨識需要 iOS 26 以上。

字幕為什麼會晚一點出現、或前面的字被改掉？
語音辨識需要聽完一小段才能判斷，句子說完後，前面的字也可能被修正。這是正常現象。

「說話者 1／2」是什麼？
App 會在手機上依聲音特徵自動分辨不同的人，在字幕和逐字稿前面標上說話者。分不清楚時就不標，也可能標錯，僅供參考。

可以把錄音檔轉成逐字稿嗎？
可以。在字幕頁按「匯入錄音檔」，選擇手機裡的錄音檔（單一檔案最長 1 小時），確認語言後就會轉成逐字稿並翻譯，可以複製、分享或存進歷史紀錄。處理時請讓 App 保持在畫面上，切到其他 App 會中斷。匯入的檔案只在處理時暫存，處理完就刪除，不會上傳。

我的對話紀錄存在哪裡？
存在你的手機上，不會傳給我們；如果你開啟 iCloud 或電腦備份，會包含在 Apple 的備份中。你可以在「設定」關閉「儲存歷史紀錄」，或按「清除全部歷史紀錄」。詳見隱私權政策。

按了「不允許」之後怎麼辦？
App 畫面上有「前往設定」按鈕，也可以到手機的「設定」→「喵喵即時口譯」重新開啟麥克風或語音辨識。

切到其他 App 後還會收音嗎？
會。未開啟錄音時最多 5 分鐘，之後自動停止；開啟錄音時會持續到你按停止，單段最長 3 小時，錄滿會自動停止並存檔。這段時間狀態列會顯示麥克風已啟用。

怎麼錄音？錄音存在哪裡？怎麼刪？
錄音預設關閉。到「設定」開啟「儲存錄音」（需先開啟「儲存歷史紀錄」），之後每次按「開始」到「停止」會存成一段錄音，可以在「歷史紀錄」回聽、分享或刪除。錄音檔只存在你的手機上，不會上傳，也不包含在 iCloud 或電腦備份中；也可以在「設定」按「清除全部錄音」。錄下他人說話前，請先取得同意，並遵守當地法律。

收音中可以回聽錄音嗎？
不行。收音中會暫停回聽，按「停止」後就可以播放。

How to use

Open the app, choose the language you hear and the language to translate into, tap Start, and allow the microphone and speech recognition. Place the phone near the sound source (for example next to the speaker of a computer in a meeting). Captions appear live with the translation below. Lighter text is still being corrected; it turns darker once it is settled.

FAQ

Can it capture audio from my computer or video-call app directly?
No. iOS does not let an app capture another app's audio, so the app can only listen through the microphone. If you join the call on the same phone, the call app may take over the microphone, so we suggest using a second device.

The first run is slow, or asks to download a language?
The first time you use a language, iOS needs to download its data, so please be online. Afterwards it works offline. Japanese and Korean recognition require iOS 26 or later.

Why do captions arrive late or change?
Speech recognition needs to hear a little before it decides, and earlier words can be corrected after a sentence ends. This is expected.

What does "Speaker 1/2" mean?
The app tells different voices apart on your phone and labels captions and transcripts by speaker. If it is unsure it leaves the label off, and it can be wrong. For reference only.

Can I turn an audio file into a transcript?
Yes. On the Captions screen tap Import Recording, pick an audio file on your phone (up to 1 hour), confirm the languages, and the app transcribes and translates it. You can copy, share or save the result to History. Keep the app on screen while it works; switching to another app stops the processing. The imported file is only stored temporarily while it is processed and is never uploaded.

Where is my history stored?
On your phone; it is not sent to us. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it is included in your Apple backup. You can turn off "Save history" in Settings or tap "Clear all history". See the Privacy Policy.

I tapped "Don't Allow". What now?
The app shows a "Go to Settings" button. You can also re-enable Microphone or Speech Recognition in iOS Settings under the app.

Does it keep listening after I switch apps?
Yes. With recording off, for up to 5 minutes, then it stops automatically. With recording on, until you tap Stop, up to 3 hours per recording; when the limit is reached it stops and saves automatically. The microphone indicator is shown in the status bar meanwhile.

How do I record? Where are recordings stored, and how do I delete them?
Recording is off by default. Turn on "Save recordings" in Settings ("Save history" must be on). Each Start-to-Stop is saved as one recording that you can play, share or delete in History. Recordings stay on your phone, are never uploaded and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. You can also tap "Clear all recordings" in Settings. Recording other people may require their consent under local law.

Can I play recordings while listening?
No. Playback is paused while the app is listening; tap Stop and you can play it.
