喵喵即時口譯 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

一句話說明：喵喵即時口譯不收集、不上傳、不分享你的個人資料。語音辨識與翻譯都在你的手機上完成；你選擇保留的字幕紀錄與錄音存在你自己的手機裡，不會傳給我們。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有我們自己的伺服器，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容，App 也不會把你的語音或文字傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。

2. 資料存在哪裡

App 可以把辨識出的原文與翻譯存成「歷史紀錄」。這些紀錄存在你的手機上，不會上傳給我們。如果你開啟 iCloud 或電腦備份，它們會包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中（見下方說明）。

  • 你可以在 App 的「設定」關閉「儲存歷史紀錄」，也可以隨時按「清除全部歷史紀錄」。
  • 歷史紀錄預設開啟，並有大小上限（預設 20 MB，最高 100 MB）；超過上限時會自動刪除最舊的紀錄。
  • 歷史紀錄是以未加密的文字檔存在 App 的資料夾內。如果你和別人開會，紀錄可能包含其他人說的話；請在需要時關閉儲存或清除紀錄。錄音檔同理。
  • 預設情況下 App 不會錄下聲音。若你在「設定」開啟「儲存錄音」，錄音檔只存在你的手機裡，不會上傳，也不包含在 iCloud 或電腦備份中；你可以在歷史紀錄刪除單段，或在設定清除全部錄音。錄音需要先開啟「儲存歷史紀錄」；按「清除全部歷史紀錄」時，歷史紀錄裡的錄音也會一併刪除。
  • 錄音有獨立的容量上限（預設 200 MB，可調整到 1 GB）；空間不足時會先刪除最舊的錄音，文字紀錄仍會保留。
  • 錄下他人說話前，請先取得同意，並遵守當地法律。
  • 你也可以匯入手機裡的錄音檔，轉成逐字稿並翻譯。匯入的檔案只在處理時暫存在手機上，處理完就刪除，不會另存或上傳；你選擇存進歷史紀錄的，只有逐字稿與翻譯文字。
  • 刪除 App 會一併刪除這些紀錄。如果你開啟了 iCloud 或電腦備份，紀錄可能包含在 Apple 的裝置備份中，由 Apple 依照你的設定處理。

3. App 會要求的權限

  • 麥克風：收聽說話聲音，即時轉成字幕。聲音用來即時辨識；只有你開啟「儲存錄音」時才會存成錄音檔，而且只存在你的手機上、不上傳。
  • 語音辨識：把聽到的英文、中文、日文或韓文轉成文字。App 要求辨識在你的手機上進行，不使用雲端辨識。iOS 要求語音辨識的權限提示固定寫「語音資料會傳送給 Apple」，那是系統對所有 App 的通用文字，不是本 App 另外傳送資料。

這些權限都可以隨時到裝置的「設定」裡關閉。未開啟錄音時，App 在你切到其他 App 後最多繼續收音 5 分鐘，之後會自動停止；若你開啟了錄音，會持續收音與錄音到你按停止為止，單段最長 3 小時。這段時間 iOS 會在狀態列顯示麥克風已啟用。

4. 語言資料與翻譯

翻譯使用 Apple 內建的翻譯功能，日文與韓文辨識（iOS 26 以上）使用 Apple 內建的語音辨識。第一次使用某個語言時，iOS 會向 Apple 下載該語言的資料，這個下載由 Apple 的系統處理，本 App 不會傳送你的語音或文字。下載完成後，辨識與翻譯都在手機上執行。

5. 第三方服務

本 App 沒有廣告、分析或追蹤類的第三方 SDK。語音辨識與翻譯使用 Apple 提供的系統功能，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。分辨說話者使用開源元件 FluidAudio（Apache-2.0）與 pyannote 模型（CC BY 4.0），模型內建在 App 裡、完全在手機上執行，不會連網或傳送任何資料；授權全文見 App 的「關於 → 開源授權與素材出處」。

6. 準確度聲明

辨識與翻譯結果可能有誤，也可能漏字，僅供溝通參考，不適合作為法律、醫療或其他需要精確內容的依據。字幕前的「說話者 1／2」是在手機上用聲音特徵自動分辨的結果，可能標錯或不標，不代表正確辨識說話者身分；這項分析只在手機上進行，不會上傳，也不會儲存聲紋。

7. 兒童隱私

本 App 不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

8. 政策更新

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

Privacy Policy for 喵喵即時口譯

Effective October 24, 2026

In short: The app does not collect, upload or share your personal data. Speech recognition and translation run on your phone. Caption history and recordings, if you keep them, are stored on your phone and are not sent to us.

1. We don't collect data

The app has no accounts, no servers of ours, and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We (小盜 Studio) cannot see anything you do in the app, and the app does not send your speech or text to us or to any third party.

2. Where your data lives

The app can save recognized text and its translation as "History". History stays on your phone and is never uploaded to us. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it is included in your Apple device backup (see below).

  • You can turn off "Save history" in Settings and clear all history at any time.
  • History is on by default with a size cap (20 MB by default, up to 100 MB); when the cap is reached the oldest entries are deleted automatically.
  • History is stored as an unencrypted text file inside the app's storage. If you are in a meeting it may include what other people said; turn saving off or clear history when needed. The same applies to recordings.
  • By default the app does not record audio. If you turn on "Save recordings" in Settings, recordings stay on your phone, are never uploaded and are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. You can delete a single recording in History or clear all recordings in Settings. Recording requires "Save history" to be on, and "Clear all history" also deletes the recordings in your history.
  • Recordings have their own storage cap (200 MB by default, adjustable up to 1 GB); when space runs out the oldest recordings are deleted first and the text history is kept.
  • Recording other people may require their consent under local law.
  • You can also import an audio file from your phone to transcribe and translate it. The imported file is copied to temporary storage on your phone only while it is processed, then deleted; it is never kept or uploaded. If you save the result to History, only the transcript and translation text are saved.
  • Deleting the app deletes this history. If you use iCloud or computer backups, it may be included in your Apple device backup, handled by Apple according to your settings.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: listens to speech to show live captions. Audio is used for live recognition; it is saved as a recording only if you turn on "Save recordings", and recordings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • Speech Recognition: converts English, Chinese, Japanese or Korean speech to text. The app requires recognition to run on your phone and does not use cloud recognition. The iOS permission prompt for speech recognition always says speech data is sent to Apple; that is generic system wording shown for every app, not a separate transfer by this app.

You can turn these off at any time in Settings. When recording is off, the app keeps listening for up to 5 minutes after you switch to another app and then stops automatically. When recording is on, it keeps listening and recording until you tap Stop, up to 3 hours per recording. iOS shows the microphone indicator in the status bar during that time.

4. Language data and translation

Translation uses Apple's built-in translation feature, and Japanese and Korean recognition (iOS 26 or later) uses Apple's built-in speech recognition. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads that language's data from Apple. That download is handled by the system; the app does not send your speech or text. Once downloaded, recognition and translation run on your phone.

5. Third-party services

The app includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Speech recognition and translation use system features from Apple, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy. Speaker separation uses the open-source FluidAudio library (Apache-2.0) and pyannote models (CC BY 4.0), bundled in the app and run entirely on your phone without any network access; see About → Open-source licenses in the app for the full license texts.

6. Accuracy

Recognition and translation can be wrong or miss words and are meant as a communication aid only, not as a basis for legal, medical or other work that needs exact content. The "Speaker 1/2" label before a caption is an automatic guess from voice characteristics made on your phone; it may be wrong or missing and does not identify who a person is. This analysis runs only on your phone; nothing is uploaded and no voiceprints are stored.

7. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the release notes.
